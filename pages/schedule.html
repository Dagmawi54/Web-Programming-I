Ticket Booking Guidelines

Please review these simple steps before planning your visit:

  1. Choose your preferred movie and screening time from the schedule above.
  2. Arrive at least 15 minutes prior to the showtime to collect your physical tickets.
  3. Concession items and refreshments are available at the front lobby snack bar.
  4. Children under the age of 3 are admitted free of charge for morning shows.
