About Our Cinema

Welcome to ABC Cinema, your premier destination for the ultimate entertainment experience. Located in the heart of the city, we bring you the latest Hollywood and international blockbusters in state-of-the-art auditoriums.

Whether you are planning a family night out or catching the midnight premiere of an anticipated blockbuster, ABC Cinema provides crystal-clear picture quality and immersive sound.

Why Choose ABC Cinema

We are dedicated to providing the best movie-going experience with modern facilities:

  • Ultra HD 4K laser projection with high-contrast screens
  • Immersive Dolby Atmos multidimensional sound systems
  • Ergonomic luxury recliner seating with generous legroom
  • Full-service concession stand offering fresh popcorn, snacks, and chilled beverages
  • Accessible auditoriums with dedicated wheelchair spaces and assistive listening devices
